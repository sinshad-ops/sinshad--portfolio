# Interview prep: explain the work honestly

Use this as a practice sheet, not as a script to memorise. The best answer is one you can support by opening the files and walking through an example.

## A clear 20-second introduction

“I’m a CSE student graduating in 2027, interested in remote AI evaluation, annotation and content-quality work. I’ve built a portfolio to practise rubrics, data checks, error analysis and review queues. Some projects use synthetic examples; two use public datasets with their original labels. I used AI tools during development, and I’m working through the cases so I can explain the choices and limitations myself.”

## Questions you should be able to answer before an interview

- Why did you choose this project, and what question were you trying to answer?
- Which files contain the input data, the method, the results and the failures?
- What does the main metric mean? What does it not mean?
- Where did the labels come from? Which are synthetic and which are public?
- Show one mistake or limitation and explain how you noticed it.
- What did you personally inspect or change? Be specific; do not guess a number.
- What would require a human reviewer or a Malayalam native speaker?
- What parts were AI-assisted, and which decisions can you explain and defend?

## Before you say you reviewed a project

Open at least ten records, including a few failures. Write down one correct result, one questionable result and one limitation in `PERSONAL-WORKBOOK.md`. Run the relevant script and tests. Only describe those steps as completed after you have done them.
